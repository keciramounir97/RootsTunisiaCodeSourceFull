\vspace{1.2em}
\noindent This is an operator's plan, not a slide deck. It assumes a genealogy product that already exists, a bilingual or multilingual audience, and a market that will punish generic consumer genealogy positioning. Roots Tunisia wins by being the most trustworthy archive-shaped product for one people---not by imitating Ancestry, MyHeritage, or a sibling Roots site.

\section{Mandate}
Preserve and make searchable the documentary memory of Tunisia families. Convert that trust into paid research seats, institutional licences, and a living archive that Google can understand.

\textbf{North-star (36 months):} become the default starting point for a serious researcher of this population---the URL a notary, a parish office, a diaspora association, or a journalist pastes without thinking.

\section{Audience architecture}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Primary:} Tunisian families in 24 governorates, the Italian and Maltese historical communities, and the Tunisian diaspora.
  \item \textbf{Secondary:} professional genealogists, local historians, university seminars, and cultural associations that need citable trees and media.
  \item \textbf{Tertiary:} Google---every public page is a landing page with a unique title, hreflang, and a sitemap entry.
\end{itemize}

Do not mix this brand with another Roots property in ads, social, or on-page copy. Shared engineering is invisible; shared taste is a leak.

\section{Positioning}
\textbf{For} families and researchers of this geography \textbf{who} cannot reconstruct a lineage from a single civil-register export, \textbf{Roots Tunisia is} the bilingual research surface that holds trees, photographs, oral history, books and articles in one catalogue. \textbf{Unlike} global consumer genealogy brands, it is built around local archives, local scripts (including RTL), and local naming practice.

\section{Offer architecture}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Free public layer:} searchable gallery, articles, about, legal. This is the SEO engine.
  \item \textbf{Member layer:} private trees, tasks, notes, reminders, download requests.
  \item \textbf{Research / institutional layer:} licensed corpora, bulk GEDCOM, teaching seats.
\end{itemize}
Price in the currency the audience actually uses. Publish a comparison table that is honest about what is not included.

\section{Channel system (what we actually buy and write)}
\subsection{Search (highest ROI)}
Submit \texttt{https://rootstunisia.com/sitemap.xml} to Google Search Console. Cluster content on: Tunisia genealogy, Tunisian family tree, Kairouan nasab, Carthage ancestry, état civil Tunisie. One article or collection landing per cluster per quarter. Internal links from About and Research. Never doorway-page the sibling countries.

\subsection{Community}
Partner with one archive, one association, and one university seminar in year one. Co-brand a digitisation day. Film oral-history clips the site can host. This is cheaper than ads and compounds.

\subsection{Paid}
Start with brand-defence Search ads on the site name only. Expand to 8--12 non-brand genealogy queries once organic titles are unique. Geo-fence to the relevant diaspora cities. Creative stays in this site's visual language.

\subsection{Email}
A monthly "record of the month" in fr plus one other locale. No product spam. Measure reply rate, not just opens.

\section{Ninety-day operating cadence}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Days 1--15: Search Console live, sitemap accepted, www 301, Core Web Vitals baseline, legal pages indexed.
  \item Days 16--45: five cluster articles, About page cited by partners, first association memorandum.
  \item Days 46--90: first paid brand campaign, first webinar, conversion of public researchers into member trees.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Risks}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Duplicate www / bare domain (already observed on several Roots hosts).
  \item Mixing Maghreb, Tunisia, Egypt and Western Sahara copy---search engines and communities both punish it.
  \item Political overclaim on Western Sahara pages. Stay with places, families, archives, languages.
  \item Shipping English-only metadata on a French-default site (Tunisia, Abraham, Western Sahara).
\end{itemize}

\section{Budget sketch (illustrative, not a quote)}
Year-one working budget should be 55\% content and partnerships, 25\% search engineering and hosting, 15\% measured paid, 5\% design production unique to this brand. Do not spend the paid line until Search Console coverage is green.

\section{Close}
Roots Tunisia is a memory institution that happens to be software. Market it that way. The sitemap, the language prefixes, the about page, and the first partnership will do more than a rebrand.
